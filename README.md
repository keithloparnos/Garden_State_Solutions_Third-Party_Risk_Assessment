# Garden State Solutions
## Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment

**Project Type:** Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)  
**Assessment Focus:** Third-Party Risk Management  
**Assessment Methodology:** Risk-Based Assessment  
**Vendor Risk Rating:** High Inherent Risk  
**Vendor Approval Recommendation:** Conditional Approval Pending Due Diligence

---

## 1. Project Overview

This portfolio project demonstrates a third-party cybersecurity risk assessment conducted for Garden State Solutions, a fictional cloud-based service provider evaluated by Tri-State Financial Services.

The assessment evaluates cybersecurity, operational, regulatory, and third-party risks associated with granting an external vendor access to sensitive customer information and business systems.

The objective is to identify potential security weaknesses, evaluate existing controls, recommend remediation activities, and establish appropriate vendor approval and monitoring requirements.

## 2. Assessment Scope

The assessment evaluates six primary risk areas:

- **Access Controls:** User authentication, privileged access, and multi-factor authentication.
- **Data Protection:** Encryption, customer information security, and file-sharing permissions.
- **Service Availability:** Backup procedures, disaster recovery, and business continuity.
- **Vulnerability Management:** Security scanning, patch management, and remediation.
- **Incident Response:** Security incident reporting, notification procedures, and response readiness.
- **Subcontractor Oversight:** Fourth-party risk management and subcontractor security practices.

## 3. Risk Assessment Methodology

A 5 × 5 risk scoring matrix was used to evaluate identified risks.

Risk scores were calculated using:

**Likelihood × Impact = Inherent Risk Score**

The assessment categorized risks as Low, Medium, or High based on their calculated scores.

Six cybersecurity and operational risks were documented in the accompanying risk register.

## 5. Key Findings

The assessment identified a High Inherent Risk profile associated with the vendor's anticipated access to sensitive customer information and its role in supporting important business operations.

Six risks were documented, with particular attention to access management, data protection, vulnerability management, and incident response.

Existing controls remain unverified because supporting evidence has not yet been provided or independently reviewed.

## 6. Vendor Risk Treatment

Conditional vendor approval was recommended pending completion of required due diligence activities.

Recommended remediation measures include:

- Enforcing multi-factor authentication.
- Verifying encryption and access restrictions.
- Validating backup and disaster recovery procedures.
- Establishing vulnerability scanning and patch management processes.
- Finalizing incident response and notification procedures.
- Strengthening subcontractor security oversight.

## 7. Ongoing Monitoring

The assessment recommends periodic vendor reassessments, security documentation reviews, incident monitoring, access reviews, and subcontractor oversight.

Significant security incidents or material changes in the vendor's risk profile should trigger additional assessment activities.
